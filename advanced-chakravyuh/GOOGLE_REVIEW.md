## Before you post

- **Post from a club account, not your personal one.** Google shows the reviewer's name
  and photo publicly. A single GDG club Google account keeps your identity out of it.
- **Post only one review, from one account.** Posting several reviews from different
  accounts (for example to plant fake flags) counts as fake engagement under Google's
  rules. The fake flags stay on the Waypoint board.
- **Post at least 2–3 days before the event.** New reviews can take time to appear
  publicly, and some get held for moderation.
- **Check it from a different Google account**, not the one that posted it. Your own
  review always shows to you ("just now · NEW") even while it's still hidden from
  everyone else. Signed out doesn't work as a check: Maps then shows only a few reviews
  and asks you to sign in before you can search or sort. On the other account:
  Purana Qila → Reviews → search `Chakravyuh`.
- **Players need a Google account** for this last step. Say so in the event briefing.
